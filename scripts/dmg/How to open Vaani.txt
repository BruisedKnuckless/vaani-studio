Vaani Studio: installing on your Mac
====================================

Needs: an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) with macOS 14 Sonoma or later, an internet
connection for the first setup, and about 15 GB of free space (engine plus AI models).

1. Drag "Vaani" onto the "Applications" folder in this window.

2. Open Vaani from your Applications folder.
   Vaani is free and open source, but it isn't notarised by Apple, so the first time
   macOS will say it "could not verify" the app. That's expected:
     a. Click "Done" (do not move it to the Trash).
     b. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
     c. Scroll down to the message about "Vaani" and click "Open Anyway".
     d. Confirm with your password or Touch ID, then click "Open Anyway" again.
   You only need to do this once.

   (Alternative for Terminal users:  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Vaani.app )

3. On first launch, click "Install the Engine". Vaani downloads its AI engine
   (about 2 GB) once. AI models download the first time you use each feature.

4. Optional: add API keys in Settings for cloud processing:
   Sarvam AI (best for Marathi/Hindi), Anthropic Claude or OpenAI (translation),
   and a free Hugging Face token for some optional on-device models.

Source code, help and updates: see the project's GitHub page.
